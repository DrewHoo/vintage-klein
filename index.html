The Vintage Tool Hunt

Pre-2016 tees and crewnecks (80s/90s preferred) from Milwaukee Electric Tools, Klein Tools, DeWalt, Husqvarna, Kubota, Craftsman, Bosch, Hitachi and friends, with no racing shirts — men's L/XL (22–24″ pit-to-pit). Age is judged by stitch, blank, tag or a printed date, never by the word "vintage" in a title.

Oct 6, 2026. Narrowed the hunt: Snap-on, Mac Tools and racing shirts are out. Added Husqvarna and Kubota; the first sweep found a single-stitch Husqvarna "Leader Of The Pack" at $39 and no vintage Kubota that fits. Every remaining card is still live. There is still no vintage Klein Tools shirt in L/XL. All prices are asks; "OBO" usually closes 10–25% under.

Recurring sweep over eBay, Depop, Etsy, Grailed, Poshmark, Mercari (US and Japan), Yahoo! Auctions Japan, Gem, Vinted and the web index; every listing judged lands in data/seen.json so it's never re-litigated. Gates: L/XL by pit-to-pit; pre-2000 by stitch/blank/origin, 2000–2015 only with a printed date or dated event — reprints, retro reissues, flag-waving "Made in America" graphics and "vintage"-in-title-only get passed.
