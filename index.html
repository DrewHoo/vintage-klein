The Vintage Tool Hunt

80s/90s tees and crewnecks from Milwaukee Electric Tools, Klein Tools, DeWalt, Snap-on, Mac Tools, Craftsman, Bosch, Hitachi, Makita and friends — men's L/XL (22–24″ pit-to-pit). Age is judged by stitch, blank and tag, never by the word "vintage" in a title.

Oct 5, 2026. Added Bosch, Craftsman, Hitachi, Hilti, Makita, Metabo, Ryobi and Husky, and swept Mercari Japan, Yahoo! Auctions Japan and Vinted for them. Real supply turned out thin: two new crewnecks and four watches. Metabo, Ryobi and Husky have no vintage apparel on the market. Also dropped four flag-waving "Made in America" cards. There is still no vintage Klein Tools shirt in L/XL. All prices are asks; "OBO" usually closes 10–25% under.
