The Vintage Tool Hunt

Pre-2016 tees and crewnecks (80s/90s preferred) from Milwaukee Electric Tools, Klein Tools, DeWalt, Craftsman, Bosch, Hitachi, Makita and friends, with no racing shirts — men's L/XL (22–24″ pit-to-pit). Age is judged by stitch, blank, tag or a printed date, never by the word "vintage" in a title.

Oct 6, 2026. Narrowed the hunt: Snap-on, Mac Tools and racing shirts are out, and their cards are gone. New today: a 90s Milwaukee "Power Dept." tee, a 1995 Black & Decker Snake Light tee. Every remaining card is still live. There is still no vintage Klein Tools shirt in L/XL. All prices are asks; "OBO" usually closes 10–25% under.
